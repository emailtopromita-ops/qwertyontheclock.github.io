Hampi

One of the oldest living temples in the world

Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage Site because of its ancient temples, forts, and other monuments. People settled in Hampi for the first time in 1ST century AD. Most of the temples in Hampi are not active, except Virupaksha temple. It is one of the oldest living Hindu temples in the world. It was created in the 7th century. Suprisingly, this temple withstood the worst attacks on Hampi's history including the invasion that destroyed the city.
